paper_assembly_scripts_2026-11-08.tar.gz

Editorial scripts and text chunks used on 2026-11-08 to assemble the three-theory edition of
Empirical Results from the previous edition: split.py (cuts the old sources into chunks/),
new/ (newly written sections), assemble.py (builds the numbered inputs), fix1.py (corrections
after the first build). Historical record only. The edition they produced is preserved in
01 Current Papers/Empirical Results/Previous PDFs/20261108_pre_pareto_review; the current
paper is the later Pareto-review edition. Do not rerun these against the current sources.

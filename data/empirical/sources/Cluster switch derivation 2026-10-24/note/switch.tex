\section{Result}
The switch is not derived from first principles. It reduces exactly, within the theory
as written, to three questions the theory leaves open: which baryons form an active
carrier's record, where a carrier's support ends, and which stock it carries. One
identification answers all three:
\begin{enumerate}
\item an active carrier's records are its condensed baryons, stars and cold gas;
\item a carrier's support ends where those records stop dominating the enclosed baryonic
mass;
\item a carrier's cells act on the stock bound to the carrier, with the binding
potential built from its records and the stock it currently carries.
\end{enumerate}
With this identification, and with the contact work drawn from the cells' own energy:
\begin{itemize}
\item the seven X-COP clusters keep their mass inside $R_{500}$, $0.3R_{500}$ and
$0.1R_{500}$ within 1.5\% of the no-contact control over 10\,Gyr; the same contacts with
cells throughout the cluster leave 55--79\% inside $R_{500}$;
\item each central galaxy's carried stock relaxes from 1.1--4.3 times its galactic-law
value to 0.57--0.85 times it, while the total stock in its support, 7--11 times the law
value, is unchanged within the particle noise;
\item isolated galaxies reach the law inside their supports as before.
\end{itemize}
The cells' energy per unit carried stock tends to $V_f^2$ in the deep regime, so the work
available to contacts is fixed by the theory where the cells are and vanishes where they
are not.

\section{What the theory already fixes}
Section~15 defines the occupation cell per active carrier,
$H_{{\rm cell},P}=k_BT_Hx_P{:}N_P{:}$ with $x_P=\sqrt{|\nabla\Phi_{b,P}|/a_0}$, and
specifies the exponential law for equilibrium active carriers. Appendix~O.3 and the
Milky-Way--LMC analysis assign each carrier a response stock $R_P$ that moves with it.
The selection functional is therefore a carrier sum,
\[
F=\sum_{P\in\mathfrak A}\int_{{\rm supp}\,P}\mathcal N_P\Theta\,
D\!\left(n_P\,\Vert\,n_{*,P}\right)d^3x,\qquad
n_P=\frac{g_{R,P}}{g_{b,P}},\qquad
\mathcal N_P\Theta=\frac{g_{b,P}^2\,n_{*,P}(1+n_{*,P})}{4\pi G}.
\]
Stock outside every support, or carried by no active carrier, does not enter $F$. A
contact that moves only such stock leaves $F$ unchanged and is never accepted, so this
stock evolves as collisionless matter.

\section{The identification and its costs}
\textbf{Records.} An active carrier is ``a retained, dynamically coherent source
record''. Stars and cold gas ($T\ll T_{\rm vir}$) carry their formation history: a
stellar distribution function is any function of the integrals, and a cold disk is far
from the Gibbs state of its potential. Shock-heated gas whose cooling time exceeds its
dynamical time relaxes toward that Gibbs state, and its density and temperature follow
from the potential and an entropy profile.

This is an identification. The intracluster medium does keep records in another sense:
metallicity gradients, cold fronts and its entropy history. The cooling criterion marks
haloes of about $10^{12}M_\odot$, the Milky-Way scale, not the galaxy--group boundary;
the boundary used here is set by enclosed-mass dominance. The X-ray ellipticals in the
radial acceleration relation of Lelli et al.\ (2017) count hot gas in the baryonic
acceleration and lie on the law, so the identification is tested there (Section~8).

\textbf{Support.} Carrier supports already meet at interfaces where one carrier's field
gives way to another's. The same rule, applied to all baryons, ends a support where
$M_{\rm rec}(<r)=M_{\rm hot}(<r)$. Hot gas has no support of its own. Enclosed mass,
rather than local density or acceleration, is a choice.

\textbf{Carried stock.} The response stock moves with its carrier. A particle is carried
when it is inside the support and bound to the potential of the carrier's records plus
its currently carried stock, found by iteration from the records alone. Binding to a
potential that already contains the law stock would presuppose the law; the
self-consistent potential does not.

\textbf{Work.} The work register is identified with the cell energy of the support.
This is a fourth identification. The register is refundable, so cooling contacts can
return energy to it.

\section{The cell reservoir}
Each cell holds mean energy $\Theta x\,n_*$, so the reservoir density is
\[
u=\mathcal N\Theta\,x\,n_*=\frac{g_b^2\,x\,n_*^2(1+n_*)}{4\pi G}.
\]
In the Rayleigh--Jeans regime $n_*=1/x$ this is exactly $u=g_ba_0(1+x)/4\pi G$. Its
leading term integrates to $a_0\int_0^RM_b\,dr\simeq a_0M_bR$. The stock the law supports
inside $R$ is $V_f^2R/G$ with $V_f^4=GM_ba_0$, so
\[
\frac{E_{\rm cell}}{M_{\rm stock}}\;\to\;\frac{G a_0 M_b}{V_f^2}=V_f^2
\]
as $R\to\infty$ in the deep regime. Integrated to the budget edge $R_{\rm cap}$, where
the law stock reaches the galaxy's stock budget:

\section{Galaxies}
An isolated galaxy carries all of its stock, so only the support edge can change the
galaxy runs. Condensed baryons of $6.9\times10^{10}M_\odot$ and a hot halo of
$2.5$--$15\times10^{10}M_\odot$ inside 250\,kpc put the Milky Way's edge at 115--490\,kpc,

\section{Tests and objections}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{X-ray ellipticals (checked).} Lelli et al.\ (2017) place nine X-ray
ellipticals on the law with hot gas counted, using the four group members (NGC~1407,
4261, 4472, 4649) only inside $4R_e$ and the others out to about $15R_e$. Over those radii
stars dominate the enclosed baryons. In NGC~720 hot gas becomes the dominant baryonic
component only outside about 200\,kpc, beyond the X-ray data at 75--80\,kpc (Humphrey et
al.\ 2011); the NGC~6482 gas fraction is 0.02 of the total mass at $0.1r_{200}$, inside its
data; the NGC~4649 analysis treats the gas inside $4R_e$ as negligible. Excluding the
hot gas from the record source lowers NGC~720's outermost points by 0.03--0.04\,dex
(gas fractions at 60 and 80\,kpc interpolated), within the scatter. NGC~1521, 1332 and
4125 lack a published gas profile at the radii used and were not quantified. The
identification predicts departures from the law beyond the radius where hot gas
dominates, about 200\,kpc for NGC~720.
\item \textbf{Weak lensing.} Isolated-lens signals stay on the law to about 1\,Mpc. That
lies beyond the budget edge with or without the switch and remains an open problem of the
selection.
\item \textbf{Groups.} The departure at radius $r$ begins where hot gas outweighs condensed
baryons inside $r$, so gas-poor groups follow the law further out than X-ray-bright groups
of the same dispersion.
\item \textbf{Centrals.} Brightest cluster galaxies and group centrals sit in supports
holding several times their law stock, consistent with the observed departures; the
runs keep that excess from the initial condition rather than predict it.
\item \textbf{History.} Stock reached clusters inside infalling galaxies and gas-rich
protoclusters. The switch keys on present thermodynamics, and its history dependence is
not modelled.
\end{itemize}

\section{Status}
Exact within the theory: the carrier sum of the selection functional, zero bias and zero
work for stock outside every support or carried by no carrier, and the reservoir
identity $E_{\rm cell}/M_{\rm stock}\to V_f^2$. Identified: condensed baryons as records,
the enclosed-mass dominance boundary, carried stock as stock bound to the self-consistent
carrier potential, and the register as the cell energy. Measured: X-COP support radii and
reservoirs. Simulated: the runs above, one seed. Lean
(\texttt{Entropic.ContactSelection}, Section~4): the Rayleigh--Jeans reservoir density,
the deep-regime identity and the core-reservoir arithmetic. Checked: the X-ray ellipticals on the
law sit inside their record-dominated supports. Open: a microscopic reason for the
record identification, the group regime, resolved member galaxies, and cosmological
assembly. An independent adversarial review of the first draft found the
law-potential binding circular, which the self-consistent binding replaces, and
corrected the work-spending and radial-acceleration-sample statements.

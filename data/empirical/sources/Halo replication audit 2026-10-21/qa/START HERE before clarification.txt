The tilted candidate survives independent phase replication.
Both new realizations retain substantial halo and stellar inclinations for eight billion years. Together with the original run, this establishes repeatability at the tested particle count within the same conditional stock and rigid-disk model. It is stronger evidence of dynamical feasibility, but it is not a proof of continuum stability or a completed prediction of the manuscript.

INTERPRETING THIS REPLICATION AUDIT

1. Which hypothesis is being tested?
The aligned static Milky Way solution and the dynamic conserved-stock
candidate are different claims. The manuscript itself says the aligned
solution fails the long-lived-tilt test. These runs do not reverse that
statement. They test an assembly-shaped stock route explicitly named in
Section 16 and Appendix O.3, with a collisionless stress closure supplied
as an additional modeling assumption.

2. What counts as progress?
A complete live calculation with positive mass, reciprocal disk torque,
accurate conservation, radial stellar motion and appreciable retained
tilts would establish conditional dynamical feasibility. Independent
phase repetitions test whether that particular result is fragile to
sampling. They cannot prove continuum convergence, stability of all
admissible initial states, or derivation from the full manuscript action.

3. What is fitted or selected?
The radial stock target and a0 are unchanged. The 43-degree stock tilt,
A=0.3 density quadrupole, pattern and orbit population were selected in
earlier exploratory construction. The stellar target also comes from the
tilted-halo problem. Positive orbit weights were fitted, then held fixed
here. Initial phase weights are adjusted within each orbit, with the
same prescribed constraint set for every seed. No final angle is fitted.
The new runs are independent phase draws conditional on that common
construction; they are not independent formation histories or orbit fits.

4. What should not be conflated?
 - Conservation error with physical drift. A conservative Hamiltonian can
   evolve away from an imperfect or unstable initial relative equilibrium.
 - Initial force accuracy with future force accuracy. Unfitted phases
   develop noticeable noise relative to the weak transverse supporting
   force, even when total acceleration errors remain much smaller.
 - Radial director stiffness with the stock's density axis. The previously
   verified elastic radial-director identity does not force the stock
   density into disk alignment. Full thermal/director response remains.
 - Position-inertia axis ratios with density-contour or potential ratios.
 - Raw second moments with streaming-subtracted dispersions. A separate
   coarse-bin diagnostic now checks the size of that distinction.
 - Positive phase-space mass with pointwise positivity of a finite
   multipole/spline reconstruction. A separate Laplacian check measures
   the latter rather than assuming it.

5. Scope of remaining physical closure
O.3 supplies positive stock continuity, a budget, and explicitly allows
velocity stress. It also states that projected transport and common
variational coupling remain completion data. The collisionless kinetic
branch is an effective realization, not a unique derivation. The disk is
a responding rigid gyroscope, not a deformable stellar disk. Finite
director/polarization response and the odd vertical force remain open.
No full Milky Way, SPARC, vertical-equilibrium or observational likelihood
refit is implied by small circular-speed-proxy shifts.

No manuscript or a0 changes are authorized or made by this audit.

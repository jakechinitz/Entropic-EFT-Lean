SUPPLEMENTARY DIAGNOSTICS ADDED DURING THE REGISTERED LIVE RUNS

The primary experiment, seeds, force law, integration settings, eight-Gyr
duration and descriptive retention flags remain exactly those in PROTOCOL.txt.
No new seed was selected and no model was retuned after seeing a trajectory.

These supplemental calculations were added while the runs were underway:

initial_stationarity.py
  Initial first and second derivatives of global and smoothly localized
  position moments in rotating coordinates. Shape-flow constraints do not
  force all higher moments to be stationary. The localized envelopes are
  not sharp shell cuts, so the derivative includes their radial transport.

temporal_diagnostics.py
  Same future-phase force diagnostic used in the previous audit, now
  applied to both new samples in the unchanged analytic potential.

stellar_streaming_check.py
  Subtract coarse local stellar velocity means in the final snapshots.
  Independent inversion-pair representatives are counted once. The count
  correction is a diagnostic, not an uncertainty estimate for correlated
  orbit phases. Both 8 and 32 angular-cell estimates are saved.

reconstructed_density_check.py
  Analytic Laplacian of the numerical multipole/spline potential, checked
  against force divergence. Positive particle masses do not automatically
  guarantee pointwise positive reconstructed density. This measures the
  artifact instead of assuming it absent. Radial and angular quadrature
  coverage and the unchecked domains are disclosed.

shape_definition_check.py
  Density-contour intercepts of the analytic stock target, to avoid
  confusing density-axis ratios with spherical-cut inertia-axis ratios.

matched_shape_reference.py and matched_shape_assessment.py
  The primary observational source, Dillamore & Sanders (2026), Section
  3.4, uses a 6-60 kpc spherical inertia cut. This supplementary comparison
  processes the adopted stellar target through the same cut and propagates
  the quoted independent Gaussian p and q uncertainties. The fixed target
  slopes and breaks are used. Our scalar tilt statistic uses the major-axis
  inclination, so it is described as a published-style summary rather than
  a reproduction of the entire observational likelihood.
  The registered 10-40 kpc outcomes are not replaced. Final instantaneous
  values and every averaged epoch are preserved, rather than selecting a
  particularly favorable time. The original reference-only assessment was
  saved before adding snapshot outputs; both versions are retained.

orientation_check.py
  At the final instantaneous snapshot, align the overall stellar azimuth
  (the disk model is axisymmetric), then compare the minor axis with the
  target. This detects noncoplanarity omitted by an inclination-only score.
  It does not incorporate an observational covariance of all orientation
  angles or transform the summary score into a calibrated probability.

disk_torque_profile.py
  Torque on thin uncoupled rings divided by their angular momentum proxy.
  Different local tendencies identify the internal torques needed for a
  coherent disk. The calculation is not a bending-mode or warp solution.

higher_multipole_check.py
  Add complete l=6 and l=8 multiplets to frozen snapshots with the same
  radial kernel. The independent harmonic implementation reproduces the
  baseline l<=4 force. Omitted modes include both finite-particle noise and
  real structure; their magnitude cannot be converted into a trajectory
  error or instability rate without new controlled evolution runs.

All these additions are diagnostics of the current model. They neither
change a0 nor edit the manuscript. None is used to discard an unfavorable
registered realization. Final conclusions must retain the force-resolution,
rigid-disk and full-action limitations even if all phase replicas survive.

Primary method source checked during this audit:

OBSERVED CLUSTER TESTS - declared before numerical reduction

Use the public X-COP radial profiles (all available clusters, with stellar-profile coverage explicitly recorded), independent MENeaCS lensing masses where matched, and the public Bullet Cluster lens model and gas components if accessible.

Fixed theory input: saturated surviving stock per original cohort baryon k=5.1760752079627395, with no per-cluster renormalization. This is the existing full-release convention, not a new microphysical derivation or an assertion that every observed cluster has saturated.

Null shortcut to test, NOT a necessary theory prediction: coextensive stock k times the current aperture baryon mass. Compare M_coext=(1+k)(Mgas+Mstar) against the uncorrected hydrostatic mass profiles. Infer the positive stock required, Mreq=Mgrav-Mbar, and eta=k Mbar/Mreq. eta is the relative baryon/stock retention for a conserved initial cohort; eta>1 can mean preferential stock loss/imported baryons, not automatic creation or impossibility. No hydrostatic mass corrected by assuming the cosmic baryon fraction will be treated as an independent test of that fraction.

Use published radial ranges, units and cosmology. No extrapolation of stellar profiles in the primary radial test. Report sensitivity to available mass reconstructions and to stellar-systematic uncertainty; no fitted effective coupling from hydrostatic data alone. Adjacent radial bins are correlated and fitted models smooth them: do not add pointwise residuals into an invented chi-square.

Independent lensing comparison: match overdensity radii using the lensing M500 and the published cosmology; interpolate gas and stars at those radii within coverage. Marginal lensing errors are not a joint likelihood. Aperture radius changes with the lensing mass and must be propagated.

Bullet: downloaded reconstructions are empirical targets, never theory predictions. Separate gas from total convergence where documented; preserve map normalization and source redshift. Galaxy halos are NOT purely stellar mass. Compare any existing merger outputs only as resolution/scale diagnostics, not as a fit to Bullet. Actual simulation initial masses, orbit, shapes, gas profile, physical contact rate and work state remain inputs until independently fixed. Do not report a coupling bound without an adequate forward model.

Save input URLs, checksums, scripts and outputs. Preserve all previous files. Four publication papers are not edited in this experiment.

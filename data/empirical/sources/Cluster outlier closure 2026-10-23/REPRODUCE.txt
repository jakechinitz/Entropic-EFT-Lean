Interpretation rules:
- The 12 positive transport maps are constructive inventory witnesses.
  They do not predict transport, energy cost, timescale or equilibrium.
- The 22 Lean lemmas verify conditional algebra and finite conservation.
  They do not verify the observations or close the constitutive dynamics.
- Common-potential pressure and contact requirements are conditional on the
  chosen mass reconstructions; none is a fitted interaction force.
- NFW and aperture masses use the same lensing data. Do not double-count them.
- The A644 minimax displacement uses marginal error bars, not a likelihood.

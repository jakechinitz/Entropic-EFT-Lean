CONSERVATIVE CLUSTER ENCOUNTER — declared before the calculation

Purpose: evolve gravitating stock, stars/galaxies and pressure-supported gas
under a common three-dimensional potential, with an explicit finite-work
contact. Test transport, conserved inventories and projected separations.
This is an idealized effective-model experiment, not a Bullet Cluster fit.

Starting profiles are two independently declared Plummer spheres, mass ratio
0.3, scale ratio 0.3^(1/3), initial separation 8 primary scale radii, transverse
offset 0.6 and relative velocity 0.8 in G=M_primary=a_primary=1 units.
No observed final lensing map is an input. Stock/baryon allocation uses the
existing full-release cohort convention; stellar mass fraction is 0.02.
Stock and stars have the isotropic Plummer distribution; gas starts with its
analytic hydrostatic temperature in the total Plummer potential. A finite
radius truncation at 6 scale radii and particle smoothing are approximations
to be assessed with isolated controls. Initial symmetry removes spurious
component centre-of-mass displacement, velocity and spin.

Gravity: identical Plummer-softened pair law for all species, including gas.
Gas: ideal gamma=5/3 conservative SPH, fixed smoothing length, symmetric
pressure forces and Monaghan viscosity. The gas is baryonic plasma, NOT the
gamma=4/3 warm capacity fluid from the preceding note. Stock remains kinetic
and can multistream. No second algebraic RAR halo is added.

Contact: local stock–baryon pairs use central relative-velocity changes and
opposite impulses, the prior two-exponential target shape, an explicit work
and heat ledger and the symmetric clock-speed gate. All nearby baryonic
partners are allowed, including those from the other initial cluster.
There is no shock flag, cohort exclusion, or galaxy-attachment rule.
Each stock particle carries one effective internal work register and bath
ledger. They are internal energies of existing material, not new halo masses.
Newtonian gravity neglects their O(energy/mc^2) mass correction. The spatial
kernel and the relative-velocity target are selected multicarrier extensions;
the old single-centre orbital equilibrium theorem is NOT assumed to hold.
The bath and register identification are effective inputs, not native spectra.

Declared controls: zero contact, active contact at multiple rates, depleted
registers with the same contact, an isolated halo, and collisionless baryons.
Evaluate particle count and timestep refinement; preserve every attempted run.
The candidate rate is common to every stock particle with accessible baryons;
local neighbour selection is normalized. The finite range is a numerical /
effective contact scale and must be reported, not hidden as a fit parameter.

Diagnostics: total mass, momentum, angular momentum, kinetic+gravity+gas+
register+bath energy, nonnegative gas energy and register/bath boundaries;
initial-cohort inventory; gas and stock projected profiles, galaxy-core
separation and observable total projected mass peaks. Tagged components are
diagnostics, not directly observed maps. The comparison epoch is first
galaxy-core pericentre plus TWO dynamical units, not a best-looking snapshot.
Report full time histories and unresolved resolution or seed dependence.

No physical relaxation rate, assembly distribution, cluster abundance or
observational likelihood is selected by these runs. A successful encounter
would establish a conditional effective mechanism, not empirical confirmation
or a uniqueness theorem. Failure or unexpected sensitivity is retained.

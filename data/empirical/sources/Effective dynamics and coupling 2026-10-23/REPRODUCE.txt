EFFECTIVE DYNAMICS AND COUPLING — REPRODUCTION

Read START HERE.html and Research note/main.pdf first. The final contact uses
the symmetric gate and the positive Hamiltonian edge completion. Earlier
one-sided gates and the exchange-only Hamiltonian remain diagnostic records.

All scripts use Python, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib; the entropy check also uses
SymPy. Note rendering uses PyMuPDF and Pillow. check_lean.py uses the available Lean/mathlib 4.19 cache under
Unified closure 2026-10-21. It does not rebuild the upstream 4.35 project.

Simulation scripts refuse to replace their named result files. To reproduce, preserve
the old output under Previous results or use a fresh research directory and
new output names. Never delete old files. No simulation runs during typesetting.

finalize.py validates saved results without rerunning simulations. It preserves
exact prior metadata and overview bytes in a timestamped source_records folder
before writing updated reporting files. The Lean checker writes timestamped
audit records. Its 36 certificates supplement, rather than rebuild, upstream.

The current kinetic_feedback.py explicitly records the bath-temperature
argument. The initial high-temperature source is saved in source_records.
Source versions, Lean audit files and reporting failures are all retained.
Random seeds are recorded in each result. Different timesteps draw different
random histories, so sampling differences are not deterministic truncation
errors. The finite interaction rates are illustrative inputs.

From the workspace root, build the PDF beside its source:
  python .organization/build_pdfs.py --tex "02 Research/Effective dynamics and coupling 2026-10-23/Research note/main.tex"
Then render the saved PDF with this directory's render_note.py and inspect
the resulting Review render folder. The build helper preserves previous PDFs.

The late scalar-only interface test is not an accepted conservative
continuation. Its resolved-energy deficit motivates the separate thermal
Euler extension. That extension conserves current and energy and retains
positive density, a causal sound speed and the floor in five resolution tests.
It chooses an isotropic gamma=4/3 thermal pressure. Its heat profile is still
resolution dependent and is not a quantitative prediction for voids. The warm
spatial and kinetic-contact tests are separate, compatible benchmarks, not
a combined self-gravitating halo calculation. Neither uses state clipping.

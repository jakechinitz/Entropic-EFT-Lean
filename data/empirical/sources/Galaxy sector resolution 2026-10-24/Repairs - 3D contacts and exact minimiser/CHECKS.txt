Repairs requested by GPT's contact-descent audit, and their checks (2026-10-24)

1. Energy diagnostic: the sign error is fixed. Energy closes to <= 6e-4 V_f^2 in every
   3-D run.
2. Exact constrained minimiser (minimizer.py): Y* = clip(weighted isotonic regression of
   T = r^2 J/G, 0, M) on a finite domain. It matched a constrained QP solver on 300 random
   cases to 3e-11 and reproduces GPT's counterexample. Negative response mass is <~1e-6 of
   the response mass, even in dense cores. When the budget binds, O.3's
   clipped-and-renormalised target has 4x (dwarf) and 13x (light-halo massive spiral)
   the minimum mismatch, so the O.3 target should be replaced by Y*.
3. Conservative 3-D contacts (descent3.py): nearest-neighbour pairs, impulse along the
   separation. Contacts conserve P and L to 1e-17; the integrator is time-reversible to
   6e-14 kpc.
4. Descent property (measured directly): E_mis is not monotone. It rises in 5-12 of 24
   half-Gyr intervals with contacts and 12-14 without; the net fall is to ~1% (MW, one
   proposal per orbit, the shot-noise level) or 6-7% (more proposals per orbit). Dwarf ends
   5-10% above the exact floor. The budget-limited massive spiral is still converging at
   12 Gyr (66 -> 4 x floor).

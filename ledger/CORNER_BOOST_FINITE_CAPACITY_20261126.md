# Corner boost and the finite-capacity premise: manuscript and formalization map

The full manuscript of 26 November 2026 adds to Q.11 the paragraph "Corner boosts of quantized general relativity",
to Section 1.1 and to the Appendix Q finite-capacity evidence paragraph a statement that the first theory-defining
input holds for vacuum causal diamonds in the Lorentzian quantization of general relativity on null cones
(V. Chandrasekaran, arXiv:2611.34924), and the same statement to input 1 of the compact account.
`Entropic/Extensions20261126/` formalizes the finite identities.

| Module | What is proved | Ledger |
|---|---|---|
| `CornerBoost` | with `A_ch = 4 ln 2 · ħG₀`, the corner boost weight continued to angle `θ`, `e^{−θA_ch/(8πħG₀)}`, equals `e^{−bθ}` with `b = ln2/(2π)` for every `θ`; the full turn gives `1/2`; on an `N`-channel sector `d · e^{−nA/(4ħG₀)} = (2^{1−n})^N` iff `d = 2^N`; such a level contributes exactly one to `Tr e^{−𝒜/(4Għ)}` | C03-44, C15-50 |

Inputs that the Lean takes as given:

- the quantization itself: boosts generated by `𝒜/(8πG)` and the corner weight `e^{−𝒜/(4Għ)}` (his eqs. 5.22, 5.23,
  5.39), and the finite dimension of the causal-diamond Hilbert space;
- the reading of his corner state's modular flow as the boost, which is an inference from `ρ_D ∝ e^{−𝒜/(4Għ)}`.

The finite-capacity premise (`TheoryInputs.finiteCapacity`, ledger C06-01) stays a premise. The external result
covers vacuum diamonds only, and does not fix the per-channel area. Not formalized: the identification of the
coarse-grained bond vacuum with the corner state, which is the remaining lattice Bisognano–Wichmann requirement.

### C09-01  Heisenberg dual of the replacement channel
- Lines: L8604-L8621
- Statement: Let rho_* be a fixed density matrix on C^n and define the linear map E_*(X) = rho_* Tr(X) on M_n(C). Its Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint E_*^dagger, defined by Tr[E_*(X) O] = Tr[X E_*^dagger(O)] for all X, O, is E_*^dagger(O) = Tr(rho_* O) I. Consequently E_*(X) = 0 for every traceless X, and every E_*^dagger(O) is a scalar multiple of I.
- Kind: A
- Difficulty: easy
- Depends on: none
- Notes: Uniqueness of the adjoint follows from nondegeneracy of the trace pairing on M_n(C).
